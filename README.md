# Slice-of-the-Pie
Organizational tool to help organize Thanksgiving planning.

## Why use this tool?
Is Thanksgiving planning with family a process that usually leads to arguments and hair-pulling? Is your kitchen clearly a "one-butt-only" kitchen that others forget about? This tool is meant to help plan things out well ahead of time, and lets everyone participate in the meal planning in the way they can without stomping on others! You can plan who is bringing mains or side dishes, when dishes are prepared (day-off or day before), what sources of heat are needed (stove top, oven, grill, AirFryer, etc.), and a central place to add recipies and notes.
