# Slice-of-the-Pie
Organizational tool to help organize Thanksgiving planning.

## Why use this tool?
Is Thanksgiving planning with family a process that usually leads to arguments and hair-pulling? Is your kitchen clearly a "one-butt-only" kitchen that others forget about? This tool is meant to help plan things out well ahead of time, and lets everyone participate in the meal planning in the way they can without stomping on others! You can plan who is bringing mains or side dishes, when dishes are prepared (day-off or day before), what sources of heat are needed (stove top, oven, grill, AirFryer, etc.), and a central place to add recipies and notes.

## Sheet Prep
1. Download the [included xlsx file](https://github.com/SwegLeaps/Slice-of-the-Pie/blob/784e5a289d3138f097d2ae8cc73564f9919c5c50/Thanksgiving%202026%20-%20V1.2.xlsx) or [this link](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1jhTetDSGDyvKcn1xHWJZL_FzOuPh46U04b1ifHN5GQk/copy) to create a copy within Google Sheets.
***Note*** If you upload the xlsx file to Google Sheets, you'll also need to add the [included js code](https://github.com/SwegLeaps/Slice-of-the-Pie/blob/784e5a289d3138f097d2ae8cc73564f9919c5c50/AppScript) into Apps Script)
2. Once the sheet is prepared, use the tab "Meals" to assign meals, add recipe links, and other notes to help determine who is cooking what, when, and how.
3. When a meal is added to the main sheet, it is automatically added to row 2 in the tab "Ingredients". There, everyone can list the ingredients for each meal within that column
4. Individual shopping lists for each person are generated once their name is added to the main tab. Within that sheet, the ingredients are listed for each meal they are assigned. From there, you can format it anyway you'd like, and download individually.
